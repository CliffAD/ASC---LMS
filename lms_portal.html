Customer Handling SOP

From the moment a guest approaches until their order is confirmed: professional hospitality, respectful communication, and undivided attention.

5

Simple Steps

100%

One-to-One Focus
The 5 Golden Steps of Interaction Interactive Flashcards
1

Greet the Customer

Welcome immediately with folded hands and a sincere "Namaste". Make direct eye contact and acknowledge without delay.

2

Understand Requirement

Inquire with relevant questions: Is it for immediate consumption or a festival/occasion? Who is the gift for? Quantity needed?

3

Take the Order

Record the exact item name, weight in grams/pieces, and box type. Never rely on assumptions or guesswork.

4

Confirm Before Finalising

Repeat the complete order clearly to the customer. Ensure travel-safe packaging is explained upfront if travelling.

5

Suggest Relevant Add-Ons

Suggest delightful pairings (e.g. fresh savouries with ghee sweets) warmly—with care, never pressure.

Standards of Excellence Mandatory Rules
✅ MUST DO
  • • Greet with a genuine smile
  • • Focus on ONE guest at a time
  • • Offer approved sampling
  • • Explain chargeable travel boxes
  • • Thank guest and invite back
❌ NEVER DO
  • • Do not argue with customers
  • • Do not guess item codes
  • • Do not leave counter unattended
  • • Do not multi-task with phones
  • • Do not forget the handover bill
Core Mantra: "The customer is the most important person in our restaurant. They are not an interruption to our work—they are the purpose of it."

Approved Estimate-to-Billing Workflow
  1. LS Logon: Sign in with personal Staff ID (e.g. 3133). Never share your credential.
  2. Estimate $\rightarrow$ New: Select Type as Estimate and verify Showroom Cashier identity.
  3. Item Input: Enter exact Item Code or search by precise name (e.g. 814 Aloo Samosa).
  4. Salesperson Verification: Input the counter salesperson code; print estimate slip.
  5. Showroom Scan: Scan the estimated bill barcode at the final register.
  6. Payment Settlement: Complete payment mode selection and issue final tax invoice.

Final Check Routine

Always perform the "3-Point Check" before the customer departs:

1. Payment Success

Check EDC card terminal screen for "APPROVED" or UPI soundbox confirmation. Never assume an unconfirmed transaction went through.

2. Item & Box Count

UrbanPiper & Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Handling Zomato and Swiggy online delivery orders. Real-time kitchen routing to maintain delivery SLA within 10 minutes.

The 4-Step Online Delivery Pipeline
1

Monitor UrbanPiper Queue

Observe the red alert notifications under the URBANPIPER tab on your LS screen. Note customer notes (e.g. "Send extra chutney / cutlery").

2

Accept Order (Ctrl+A)

Click green Accept Order. Confirm the prompt to dispatch kitchen ticket to KDS station: ONLINE CHAATS.

3

Mark "Food Ready"

Once packed by the kitchen team, select the order and click Food Ready. This alerts the delivery rider outside.

4

Rider ID Match

Match the last 4 digits of the Order ID with the rider’s app screen before handing over the parcel.

Problem Handling: The Recovery Compass

Turning a customer complaint into lifelong brand loyalty. Master the four sacred steps: Listen • Apologise • Satisfy • Thank.

The Golden Recovery Flow
👂 1. LISTEN

Listen patiently without interruption. Validate their experience with calm, reassuring eye contact.

🙏 2. APOLOGISE

Say with sincerity: "We are truly sorry for the inconvenience, Sir/Ma'am. Let me resolve this immediately."

🍽️ 3. SATISFY

Replace the order promptly. Have the manager invite them to the dine-in area and serve fresh chaat on the house.

🤝 4. THANK

Thank the customer for highlighting the issue. Invite them back with warmth and appreciation.
